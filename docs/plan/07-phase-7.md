# Phase 7 plan: age-tiered UI and Mithu

Status: built, waiting for testing with children ([checklist](07-test-with-children.md)). Plan approved 2026-10-03.
Spec: [master-prompt.md](../master-prompt.md), C1 (age tiers), C2 (Mithu), C3 (engagement rules), C4 (mockups) and Part F, Phase 7.

**Goal:** the app feels alive and right for each age.
**Done when:** a 5-year-old can complete a counting lesson without reading, and a Grade 9 student finds the home screen clear and not childish (tested with real children).

## What exists now

- Four age tiers (`early` Nursery–2, `primary` 3–5, `middle` 6–8, `older` 9–12). Each has its own colours and text sizes, picked from the student's grade.
- One home screen for everyone: today's plan, lessons, practice and review.
- Mithu is a placeholder: a bird icon next to a speech bubble. Lessons already have `character` blocks (`emotion`, `say`, and an empty `audio` slot).
- No sound, no badges, no celebration screen, and no lesson for children who can't read.

## How it will work

### Mithu (task 7.1)

- **Mithu is drawn in code**, as a rounded, shaded green parakeet with the pink and black neck ring (decision 1). It needs no image files, so it works offline and costs almost nothing in app size.
- **Eight moods**, each with its own pose and small movement:
  - idle: blinks and sways;
  - talking: beak moves with the voice;
  - happy: hops;
  - thinking: looks up, wing to chin;
  - encouraging: thumbs-up wing;
  - surprised: feathers up;
  - celebrating: flaps and confetti;
  - curious: head tilt and a "?" (decision 2).
- **Where Mithu's mood comes from:** a lesson's `character` block sets it. Answers move it too: right → happy, wrong → curious, then encouraging. Mithu is never disappointed.
- **Less movement:** when the "Less movement" setting or the phone's own setting is on, Mithu changes pose without moving.
- **Later swap:** everything goes through one `MithuController`, so a Rive file made by an artist can replace the drawing later without touching lessons or screens.

### Voice (tasks 7.2 and 7.3)

- **Recorded lines.** Every Mithu line can have a recording per language (the `audio` field). While it plays, Mithu's beak follows the loudness of the voice. This is the "simple mouth sync".
- **Recording.** A **narration recorder** screen in the dev app lists every line without a recording. You or a teacher tap **Record**, speak, listen back, and save. One button exports the recordings, and I add them to the lessons (decision 3).
- **No recording yet.** The phone's own text-to-speech reads the line (Urdu if the phone has it, otherwise English). This is labelled so we know which lines still need a real voice.
- **Speaking rules.**
  - For Nursery–Grade 2, Mithu speaks every instruction automatically, and a big speaker button repeats it.
  - Older tiers tap to listen.
  - A line is at most 20–30 seconds; longer ones are flagged by the lesson validator.

### Sounds and animation (task 7.4)

- **Sound effects:**
  - tap, put in basket, right, try again, and celebrate;
  - generated by a script, so there are no licence questions, and tiny.
- **Who hears them:**
  - on by default for Nursery–Grade 5;
  - off by default for Grades 6–12;
  - a **Sounds** switch in Settings.
- **Animation:** playful for the young tiers and calm for the older ones. Every animation respects "Less movement".

### Home screens per tier (tasks 7.5–7.7)

| Tier | Home screen |
|---|---|
| Nursery–2 | **Village path:** a winding path through a village, each lesson a stop (house, well, mango tree) with up to 3 stars. Mithu greets the child by name and says what's next. One big **Play** button. No reading needed: every button has a picture and Mithu says it aloud. |
| Grades 3–5 | **Quests:** a story quest card (for example "Help Ali's family share rotis": a chain of lessons), subject tiles showing how many skills are secure, and effort badges. Mithu gives one tip. |
| Grades 6–8 | **Challenges:** today's plan as a short list of challenges, levels per subject (from mastery), and a badge collection. Mithu small and optional. |
| Grades 9–12 | **Clean and focused:** today's 15-minute plan, mastery by subject, a **board exam countdown** (the exam date is set once in Settings), review due and AI notes. Mithu off by default. |

Every student, at any tier, can switch Mithu on or off and change the age style in Settings.

### Badges for effort, not streaks (task 7.8)

- **Badges reward effort and understanding:**
  - finished a first lesson;
  - tried again after a mistake;
  - explained something in your own words;
  - fixed a mistake the photo checker found;
  - came back for a review;
  - practised on 5 different days this month (not in a row).
- **No streaks, no "you missed a day", no countdowns to lose something.**
- Badges are saved with the student's progress and sync like the rest.

### Celebration (task 7.9)

At the end of a lesson:
- Mithu celebrates.
- Up to **3 stars**, for effort, not just right answers: finishing, trying again, and explaining.
- One praise line.
- **One clear next step:** the next lesson, or "come back tomorrow for a review".

### A counting lesson for children who can't read (task 7.10)

- **New lesson:** "Count the mangoes" (Nursery, numbers 1 to 5).
- **New step types:**
  - `tap_count`: tap mangoes to drop them into a basket while Mithu counts aloud;
  - `pick_picture`: choose the number from big picture cards, such as dots, the digit and fingers.
- **Without reading:** every instruction is spoken, so a child who can't read can finish it. Right and wrong are shown with Mithu's reaction and a sound, not text.

## Decisions (tell me if you want any changed)

1. **Mithu is drawn in code now, not in Rive.** The spec says Rive, but a Rive character has to be drawn by an artist in the Rive editor; I can't make a good one in code. The code-drawn Mithu has all the moods and can be swapped for a Rive file later without changing lessons.
2. **"Curious" is an eighth mood.** The lessons already use it, and the spec wants Mithu to react to wrong answers with curiosity. This closes that open question.
3. **Real voices are recorded in the app** by you or a teacher, with the phone's text-to-speech until then. A generated AI voice would need a paid account, and children connect better with a real Pakistani voice.
4. **Stars count effort**, not perfect answers, following the spec's engagement rules.
5. **Mithu is off by default for Grades 9–12** and small for Grades 6–8, with a switch for anyone.
6. **The board exam date is entered once** in Settings, rather than built into the app, because dates differ between boards and years.

## What was built (notes)

- **Stars and badges are worked out from the saved answers**, so they sync with them and come back on a new phone; no new storage.
- **Stars:** finished; kept trying (tried again after a mistake, or needed no second try); explained in own words (in lessons with nothing to explain: answered every question).
- **Badge "fixed a mistake the photo checker found"** counts trying again after the photo check, because the app saves attempts, not whether the last try was right.
- **Recordings** are files named `<lesson>__<step>_<language>.m4a` in `assets/audio/`, each with a `.mouth` file the recorder makes. The microphone is only in debug builds.
- **New lessons in the daily plan now come from the student's own age group**, so older students aren't given the Nursery lesson.
- **Sounds, Mithu on/off and the exam date are per child** on a shared phone.
